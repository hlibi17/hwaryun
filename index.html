About the Character

A master strategist who plays 4D chess with destiny, Hwa Ryun is the Tower’s most enigmatic Guide. Known as the 'Red-Haired Witch,' she moves through the shadows of the Tower not with raw power, but with the chilling ability to see the future's many paths. Unapologetically blunt and fiercely loyal to her own hidden convictions, she proves that the person who truly understands the 'Path' is far more dangerous than the one wielding the strongest weapon.
